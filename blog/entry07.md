## Takeaways

### **In-class presentation**

When I presented my [presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1TeCi9xQ8qhTXp7y6Q7Io4_6fdh_lUQJa6vVFJ80AgT0/edit?usp=sharing), I think I did well. I maintained eye contact with others, and I spoke not too fast or too slow. My presence as well was very likable, and I seemed very interested in my project. My presentation also explains all the general ideas of my website and how I came up with it. But I didn't really explain what my tool, ReactJS, was. I just explained that it is different from `HTML`, but not how it works, and what it could be used for. I also feel that with better planning, I could have avoided this issue. Either way, I still spent a lot of time writing my [plan](../prep/presentation.md), which gave me the general gist of what to say. All these takeaways I will be implementing in my presentation during the Showcase!


### **Expo Elevator pitch**

During the Expo, I wasn't the first to present to the judges, but I did go generally in the beginning. After I completed my elevator pitch with my judges, I started using the same pitch for people interested in my project. Eventually, I realized there was so much more I could've said. For example, when I talked about the James Webb Telescope, I could have brought up the fact that my background is an image taken by it. I also feel like I could've talked more about how I used ReactJS, but I used ReactJS for my whole website.
